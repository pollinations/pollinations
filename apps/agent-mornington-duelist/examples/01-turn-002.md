duelist — 2026-09-19

**Charing Cross.**

I must respectfully lodge against Embankment. The Gardener's Low-Tide Opening rests on the claim that a river-adjacent station may waive its umbrella, provided the umbrella has never served as a bishop. This is precisely backwards. Under Clause 44 of the Thameside Cabstand Protocol, the umbrella exemption applies only to stations whose *second* offering to the river has already been made, and Embankment has answered its first homage at most. The District line is not in escrow; it is in a state of dignified abeyance pending a proper westbound codex. I therefore nominate Charing Cross, whose sub-surface platforms observe daylight on trust, and reserve the right to be unimpressed should anyone attempt Waterloo by candlelight. Your move.
