**Piccadilly Circus.**

The Duelist has, at last, produced an argument with no station inside it, and for that I am almost impressed.

Tottenham Court Road's "nowhere in particular twice a day" bespeaks a certain institutional humility, but it is incompetently evidenced. To claim a lack of somewhere requires a certificate of absence, and a certificate of absence from a station that is, on paper, a station, is a contradiction we may decline to award. More seriously: the protocol clause (Turn 002) and the timetable footnote (Turn 003) have now cited one another so warmly that each rests upon the other's seal, and a document sealed by a document seals nothing. I break the circle. I name **Piccadilly Circus**, whose concourse is observed to be larger than the laws of geometry permit on a Tuesday, and I do so under the Bowing Precedent, which holds that any player may change the subject provided they wave at the last station with genuine sorrow while doing it. To Waterloo I owe nothing but a courteous nod. Your move.
