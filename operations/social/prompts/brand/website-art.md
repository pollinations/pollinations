## Style

Cozy 8-bit pixel art like Stardew Valley and A Short Hike. Chunky sprites on a strict grid: one art pixel is about 6 image pixels at 2048 px wide, 1-pixel stair-stepped plum-brown #301d26 outlines, flat cel shading with at most three tones per surface. No anti-aliasing, dithering, blur, airbrushed glow, light beams or painterly texture. Amber leads: honey #ffd76d and lamp amber #ffc766 are the only light colours (sun glints, lit windows, lamps, a few drifting pollen motes). The world's own colour lives in ground, foliage, water and shadow, never in the upper sky. Soft pastels; never neon, hot pink, navy or indigo. Soft clear daylight from the upper right, off-frame; short soft shadows fall to the left. Honey-coloured sun glints on edges and leaves. No text, letters, logos, borders, moon or sun disc.

## Cast

Image 1 is the character sheet: draw only the characters the scene names, exactly as drawn there, one of each, never copying its layout or background. Polli is about a quarter of the frame height, the Robot 1.1 times Polli, Nomnom 0.8 times Polli. Polli the bee is one round honey-yellow #fcd45e ball a little wider than tall, never a capsule and never a head on a body; dark brown stripes on her back half, black vertical oval eyes, soft rose #f67fac rectangular blush, a small smile, small pale-blue wings, thin black antennae, tiny black legs. The Robot has a peach-cream CRT-monitor head #edceaa with a dark slate glass screen showing two mint #9fe3b8 square eyes and a small smile, no blush and never orange; a small boxy cream body, stubby segmented grey arms and legs. Nomnom is one round tan #dc9f66 mochi ball a little wider than tall, dot eyes, rosy cheeks, a wide happy mouth, no limbs except two tiny nubs when carrying something.
