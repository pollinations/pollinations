Publication requires approval of this agreement and verification of the provider register, transfer safeguards, and operational measures in its annexes. This draft does not certify those arrangements as already implemented.

## 1) Parties, acceptance, and scope

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of Service](/terms) between the customer identified by its Pollinations.ai account ("Customer") and **Myceli.AI OÜ**, registry code 17186693, Tornimäe tn 5, 10145 Tallinn, Estonia ("Pollinations"). On publication of the approved version, acceptance of the Terms incorporates this DPA without a separate signature. It applies when Pollinations processes personal data in API prompts, messages, uploaded media, outputs, or related request data on Customer's behalf ("Customer Data"). Customer is controller, or processor acting on an authorised controller's instructions; Pollinations is processor, or sub-processor respectively. GDPR terms have their GDPR meanings.

Pollinations' independent controller activities, including account administration, payments, accounting, fraud prevention, and its own usage analytics, are described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy). This DPA does not change those roles. Independently operated community endpoints and customer-selected third-party apps are described separately in the provider register; this DPA does not attest to their practices or establish a processing agreement with them. Pollinations remains responsible for its own processing and for providers it engages as sub-processors.

This DPA prevails over conflicting Terms concerning Customer Data. Mandatory law and any applicable transfer clauses prevail over this DPA. The Terms otherwise continue to apply, without restricting statutory rights of data subjects or supervisory authorities.

## 2) Instructions and permitted processing

Pollinations will process Customer Data only on Customer's documented instructions, including its API requests, selected models, settings, and this DPA, to provide and secure the requested service. International transfers are also subject to those instructions and section 6. Where Union or Member State law requires other processing, Pollinations will inform Customer before processing unless that law prohibits notification on important public-interest grounds.

Pollinations will immediately inform Customer if an instruction appears to infringe applicable data-protection law and may suspend the affected processing while the parties resolve it. Customer is responsible for its lawful basis, notices, authority to disclose the data, and instructions. Customer must minimise submitted personal data and must not submit special-category or criminal-offence data without a separate written agreement covering the necessary safeguards.

Pollinations will not use Customer Data to train or fine-tune models without Customer's separate, explicit instruction. Provider-specific training, abuse-review, logging, and retention terms must be verified for the selected route, including its fallbacks; no provider-wide zero-retention or no-training certification is made by this draft.

## 3) Confidentiality and security

Pollinations will ensure that people authorised to process Customer Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and have access only as needed for their duties. It will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures under GDPR Article 32, taking account of the nature, scope, context, purposes, and risks of processing. Annex B describes the measures to be confirmed before this DPA becomes effective.

Pollinations will regularly assess the effectiveness of those measures and maintain procedures for restoring access and availability after incidents. Changes must not materially reduce the protection of Customer Data.

## 4) Assistance and personal-data breaches

Taking account of the nature of processing, Pollinations will assist Customer with data-subject requests through appropriate technical and organisational measures. It will forward requests relating to Customer Data without undue delay and will not respond substantively except on Customer's instructions or as required by law. Customer should send requests to **hello@pollinations.ai**, identifying the account and relevant request or media identifiers; do not send credentials or unnecessary personal data.

Taking account of the information available to it, Pollinations will assist Customer with security obligations, breach notifications, data-protection impact assessments, and prior consultation under GDPR Articles 32–36. Assistance includes relevant processing and security information and cooperation in identifying, exporting, restricting, or deleting affected Customer Data where possible.

Pollinations will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Customer Data. The notice will describe the nature of the breach, affected data and individuals where known, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed, and a contact for follow-up. Incomplete information may be supplied in stages without undue further delay. Pollinations will cooperate in containment and remediation; Customer remains responsible for its notifications to authorities and individuals.

## 5) Sub-processors

Customer gives general written authorisation for the verified sub-processors listed in the [Sub-processor Register](/subprocessors), for the purposes and locations disclosed there. Entries marked unverified in this draft are an investigation inventory, not an approved list.

Before engaging a new or replacement sub-processor, Pollinations will give affected Customers at least **14 days' prior notice** through their account contact or an in-product notice that identifies the change and links to its details. Merely editing the register is not notice. Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period by contacting **hello@pollinations.ai**. The parties will work to resolve the objection; if they cannot, Customer may stop the affected processing and terminate the affected service before the change takes effect, with a refund of unused prepaid fees attributable to that service.

Pollinations will impose substantially the same data-protection obligations on each sub-processor by written contract, verify sufficient guarantees, and remain fully liable to Customer for that sub-processor's performance of its obligations. It will provide information about downstream sub-processors needed for Customer's assessment on request.

## 6) Locations and international transfers

Processing locations depend on the selected route and its fallback chain, as recorded in the provider register. This DPA does not promise EEA-only processing. Customer must confirm that the documented locations meet its requirements before submitting personal data.

Before transferring Customer Data outside the EEA, Pollinations will establish and document an applicable GDPR Chapter V safeguard, such as an applicable adequacy decision or the European Commission's transfer Standard Contractual Clauses under Decision (EU) 2021/914, with the correct module, completed annexes, transfer assessment, and supplementary measures where required. Pollinations will make relevant safeguard information available to Customer on request, with appropriate redactions.

This DPA is an Article 28 agreement. It does not itself execute transfer SCCs with providers or claim that the Article 28 clauses in Decision (EU) 2021/915 are a Chapter V transfer mechanism. A route whose necessary safeguards cannot be established must not be represented as covered for personal-data processing under this DPA.

## 7) Return, deletion, and duration

This DPA lasts for the service term and any period in which Pollinations retains Customer Data as processor. At the end of the affected service, Customer may choose return or deletion of Customer Data by contacting **hello@pollinations.ai**. Pollinations will provide available data in a commonly used electronic format and delete active copies within 30 days after termination or the agreed return, unless Union or Member State law requires retention. Customer may request confirmation of completion.

Restricted backup copies will expire through the documented backup cycle, will not be used for other purposes, and, if restored, will remain subject to the deletion instruction. Legally required retention will be limited to the necessary data and duration, with Customer informed where permitted. Sub-processors must follow equivalent obligations.

Annex A distinguishes processor data from independently retained controller records. Routine cache expiry is not a deletion guarantee for public or customer-controlled copies; customers should avoid public delivery of personal data.

## 8) Information and audits

Pollinations will make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow and contribute to audits, including inspections, by Customer or an auditor it mandates. The parties will first use relevant documentation where sufficient, and arrange inspections on reasonable notice with safeguards for other customers' data, confidentiality, and service availability. Urgent incidents, reasonable evidence of non-compliance, or a competent authority's requirement may justify shorter notice. These arrangements must not prevent an effective audit or statutory oversight.

### Retention and delivery

The following reflects the published Privacy Policy and must be reconciled with operational and provider evidence before publication of this DPA:

- Generated response caches: **typically up to 30 days**. This is not a universal maximum for provider logs or downstream public/browser caches.
- Uploaded media: a **30-day lifecycle from upload or latest refresh**. A body retrieval refreshes it when the upload is at least 15 days old; actively accessed files can remain longer.
- Provider prompt, output, safety-review, and request-log retention: **route-specific; verification pending in the register**. No blanket 30-day limit is established.
- API usage metadata retained for Pollinations' controller purposes: **typically 24 months**; billing, wallet, and tax records: **up to 7 years where required**. These are not inference-content retention promises.
- Restricted backups: published policy states a **30–90-day rotation**; coverage and enforcement must be confirmed.

Image requests may use **POST /v1/images/generations** with a JSON body instead of placing the prompt in the caller's URL. POST does not guarantee that internal routing, provider systems, image metadata, telemetry, or caches contain no prompt-derived data. The standard service offers no verified zero-logging or private-cache guarantee in this draft.
